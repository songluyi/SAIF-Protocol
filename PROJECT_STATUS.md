# SAIF Protocol Project Status

**Status: PAUSED / FROZEN**  
**Effective date: 2026-08-16**

SAIF Protocol is intentionally paused. The repository, specification history, branches, and prior research are being preserved, but active feature development and protocol expansion are suspended.

## Why the project is paused

1. **No validated customer or revenue pull yet.** The project does not currently have paying users, committed production adopters, or a sufficiently strong commercial pull to justify continued primary development effort.
2. **Opportunity cost is too high.** Current development capacity is better allocated to projects with existing customers, near-term revenue, regulated execution barriers, or professional-service automation where commercial validation can happen faster.
3. **Agent-payment infrastructure is moving quickly.** Major payment and commerce infrastructure providers are increasingly building agent-initiated payment and commerce capabilities. SAIF should not spend scarce resources competing to recreate payment rails that larger providers are structurally better positioned to operate.
4. **The differentiated long-term thesis needs revalidation.** If SAIF resumes, the stronger candidate is a vendor-neutral financial authority and control layer: delegation, mandate, policy, limits, approvals, evidence, revocation, and audit across multiple payment or execution rails. That thesis must be validated by real integrators or customers before material new development resumes.
5. **Commercial sustainability comes first.** Long-horizon protocol work should be funded by customer demand, dedicated capital, or profitable operating businesses rather than displacing projects that can generate cash flow now.

## Freeze policy

While frozen:

- no new protocol versions or scope expansion;
- no new commerce, wallet, payment-rail, marketplace, or provider roadmap work;
- no speculative feature development;
- no claim that SAIF is production-ready;
- existing open development PRs are closed without merge and their branches/history are preserved;
- critical security fixes, dependency-risk fixes, factual documentation corrections, and preservation work remain permitted.

The pre-pause `main` snapshot is preserved at:

`archive/pre-pause-2026-08-16`

## Conditions to resume

Active development should resume only when at least one strong external validation signal exists, such as:

- a paying customer with a concrete SAIF authority/control requirement;
- a committed AI, commerce, payment, banking, or enterprise integration partner;
- dedicated funding or staffing that does not displace higher-confidence revenue projects;
- repeated real-world demand for cross-rail agent delegation, mandate, policy, approval, revocation, evidence, or audit;
- a validated differentiated role that is not merely a duplicate payment rail.

A restart should begin with customer discovery and a narrow commercial validation plan before reopening the technical roadmap.

## Interpretation

Paused does not mean abandoned. The protocol work remains preserved as an option for a future market in which AI agents require interoperable authority, policy, and audit controls. The current decision is a capital-allocation and execution-priority decision based on present commercial evidence.
