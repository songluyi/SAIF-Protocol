# Authorized Economic Identity Model

## Why AI Needs Authorized Economic Identity

AI Agents do not currently require independent legal personality to participate in economic activities.

The practical path is not to create AI as a new legal person, but to enable AI Agents to act as authorized economic agents under the delegation of humans and organizations.

## Core Principle

AI Agent ≠ Legal Person

AI Agent = Authorized Economic Agent

A human or organization remains the legal and economic owner. The AI Agent executes activities within explicitly defined permissions.

## Why This Matters

As AI Agents become capable of performing real-world tasks, they require:

- Verifiable identity
- Owner attribution
- Permission boundaries
- Spending controls
- Transaction accountability

The key question is not whether AI can become a legal entity today.

The key question is:

How can humans safely delegate economic actions to AI?
